Section 3

How To Use

For Spark builder

  1. Click Open Spark Interface.
  2. Read the screen.
  3. Do not rewrite the app.
  4. Do not convert to React/Vite.
  5. Use the mock buttons only.
  6. Wait for next assignment before editing production Spark files.

For TradeCore

  1. Open the canonical TradeCore app.
  2. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Use Creation Space for future change requests.
  4. Keep the same app URL and saved browser account.
  5. Prices and orders remain simulated.
  6. Real trading integrations require separate approval.
